# Violin rendering fixture

`violin_10k.splat` is distributed under **CC0-1.0** according to its [per-file source metadata](https://github.com/marcelpadilla/splats/blob/main/data/violin/meta.json). The original metadata is preserved in `source-meta.json`. [CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Source mesh: **Virtual Museums of Malopolska**, via [odedstein-meshes](https://github.com/odedstein/meshes/tree/master/objects/violin). Splat conversion: **Marcel Padilla**, [splats collection](https://github.com/marcelpadilla/splats), data snapshot 2026-08-11.

Downloaded October 17, 2026 from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marcelpadilla/splats/main/data/violin/violin_10k.splat`.

- Bytes: 320,000
- Gaussians: 10,000
- SHA256: `6cb5a33059da30752a49324ab6f84721bb29527bac3810e0856283f0ab59a6dc`
- Format: standard fixed-width 32-byte `.splat`, without spherical harmonics.

This is a mesh-to-splat conversion with baked matte shading, not an image-trained reconstruction. Its scale is normalized, not metric, and the source provides no capture cameras. The Cha Waves manifest preserves unknown world registration and an empty camera list. It is a parser/rendering fixture, not acoustic or camera-reconstruction evidence. Its CC0 terms are separate from this repository's MIT license.
